**Playtest notes**
- The game loads and renders. There are no console errors, and the animation runs at about 13 frames per second in the headless test (65 frames in 5 s).
- The menu is clear. It explains every object and every control on screen.
- The `gameState` hook stays in sync. The bot started a game, scored, lost at about 21 s, and restarted.
- The random bot scored at most 2, so the early game is punishing for a player who isn't paying attention. That is acceptable for an arcade game.
- The idle run never reached game over. The report doesn't say whether that run pressed start, so I can't tell whether the game is too lenient.
- Missing an orb summons a spike. This is a good rule, but spikes can spawn quickly around the player right at the start.
- The player can be hit the moment a game begins, with no grace period.
- The HUD is a bit cramped at top right. The dash bar sits just under the lives text and its label is small.
- The pause overlay, combo and extra-life system, golden orbs, and chaser spikes add variety.
- Difficulty ramps through spawn rate, spike speed, and chasers, which fits the theme.

**Changes I made**
- Added a short invulnerability window at the start of a run, so the player isn't hit immediately.
- Added a "GET READY" flash during that window.
- Moved the dash bar and its label so they no longer crowd the lives text.
- Made the player's blink while invulnerable and the pause logic safer.
