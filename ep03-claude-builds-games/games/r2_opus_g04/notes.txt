## Playtest notes

1. It works: the game loads, the menu is clear, input and score both respond, you can lose, restart works, the test hook is right and there are no console errors. All 8 checks passed.
2. The menu (menu.png) looks good. It has a legend for every object, the controls, and the goal of 1000 points to win.
3. **Bug:** hazards fly over the top bar. In f01.png a spike mine sits on the "Lives" icons, and in end.png another is over the score. Hazards bounce off the canvas edge at y=0, but orbs only spawn below the bar.
4. **Same bug for the player:** your ship can move up into the top bar, where the score and combo text hide it.
5. The combo text and its timer bar hang below the top bar, into the play area (y≈48–71). The playfield has no clear top edge.
6. The bot only reached 96 points in 55 s. The orbs' pickup radius is tight, so you need to steer precisely. A little magnet pull would make collecting feel better without making the game easier to survive.
7. Levels only add more and faster hazards, so a level-up brings nothing to look forward to, just more danger. It could use a reward moment.
8. Orbs that fade out vanish with a tiny burst. That's fine, but you rarely see the big orbs (12% chance, 5 s life).
9. Hazards and the player aren't drawn on the game-over screen, so you can't see what killed you. The cause of death text partly makes up for it.
10. The page footer only mentions WASD, but the code also accepts ZQSD (useful on AZERTY keyboards).
11. In the idle run the game stayed on the menu (it only starts on input), as expected.

## Changes

- I added a real playfield edge (`TOP`, with a line under the top bar). The player, hazards, lasers, orbs, warnings and the shield power-up all stay below it. Hazards coming in from the top are drawn clipped, so they slide in from under the bar.
- I made the top bar smaller: the combo counter and its timer now sit next to the score bar, inside the bar.
- Orbs near the player get pulled in slightly.
- **Orb shower:** each level-up drops a ring of 6 short-lived bonus orbs, with their own message and sound.
- Hazards stay visible, dimmed, behind the game-over screen, and a ring marks where you died.
- The footer now mentions ZQSD.
