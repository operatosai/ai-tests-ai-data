**Playtest notes**
- The game loads and renders. The menu is clear and explains the controls. There are no console errors, and the `gameState` hook updates correctly.
- Input, scoring, losing and restarting all work. The bot lost at about 23s with a score of 1.
- The idle run never reached game over inside the test window. Idling is safe for a long time, because the only things that can hurt a passive player are orb expiry and the spikes bouncing around.
- The early game is too harsh. Three orbs expire together and each costs a life. Spikes spawn aimed at the player, and the screenshot at level 2 already shows about 9 spikes.
- The orb countdown ring is thin and hard to read. The screenshot shows only a tiny arc, so the "missed orb costs a life" rule feels unfair.
- Mixing life loss on orb expiry with spike hits is an interesting idea, but the first 20 seconds give the player little time to learn it.
- Dash, combo, the golden orb, chasers and the telegraphed spawn warnings are good additions. Pause and mute work.
- The HUD is fine, but "SHIFT dash" sits right under the lives and dash bar and looks cramped.

**Changes made**
- Orbs last longer, and each orb's life is staggered so they don't all expire at once.
- The countdown ring is thicker and brighter, and it turns red as the orb is about to expire.
- The spike hitbox is a bit more forgiving.
- Invulnerability after a hit is longer.
- The hazard speed ramp is gentler early on.
- The first hazard spawns later.
