**Playtest notes**
- It loads, renders and has no console errors. The menu is clear and explains the controls.
- The bot reached "over" at about 25s and restart worked. The state hook updates correctly.
- The idle run never lost in 60s. Hazards are aimed with a spread and bounce, so standing still in the middle is nearly safe. That's a design hole.
- Orbs never expire, so there is no pressure to move and no reason to take risks.
- The difficulty ramp is only more and faster spikes. There is no variety, and the spikes appear with no warning, directly at the edge.
- There is no pause, no best score, and no combo or skill reward.
- Score is only a count to 40, and the win/loss screens are bare.
- In the screenshots the HUD "LIVES" hearts are small and a bit hard to read.
- Sound is minimal but works.

**Changes**
- Spawn warnings: a marker shows at the edge before each spike appears.
- Chaser spikes: purple spikes that home in on the player from level 3. They also punish idling.
- Orbs: they now expire after about 7s and blink before they do. Collecting several quickly builds a combo.
- Best score: stored in localStorage, with a fallback if storage isn't available.
- Pause: press P or Esc. The game also pauses when the window loses focus.
- Speed boost: Shift gives a short dash, with a cooldown bar.
- Hook: it is synced every frame.
- Hazards: capped at 14.
