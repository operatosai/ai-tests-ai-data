**Playtest notes**
1. It loads, renders and shows no console errors. The `gameState` hook stays in sync, and menu → playing → over → restart all work.
2. The menu explains controls, spike types and golden orbs clearly. The HUD is readable in the screenshots.
3. The random bot lost at about 27s with a score of 1, so the early game is fairly tough for a player who isn't paying attention.
4. Spikes never leave. They bounce forever and pile up to 14 (see the end screenshot, with spikes clumped together). Late rounds become cluttered instead of cleanly harder.
5. Missed orbs cost a life, and a hit costs another. With few lives, the game can end for reasons that feel unfair.
6. Orb pickup is tight for mouse and touch play, because the hit radius is only the two radii added together.
7. Spawn warnings (the "!" rings) and the shrinking orb timer rings are good readability touches.
8. A win at 40 points looks hard to reach, but the combo-heal mechanic gives skilled players a real path.

**Changes made**
- Spikes now have a lifetime of about 18–28s. They pop in, shrink and burst away at the end, so the screen stays readable while spawn rate and speed keep rising.
- Orb pickup radius is slightly more generous.
- Hazard collision now scales with the spike's current size, so a spike that is appearing or fading is less deadly.
