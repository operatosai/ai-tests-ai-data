**Playtest notes**

1. The bot scored all 8 of 8 checks. The game loads, starts, scores, can be lost (game over at about 35 s) and restarts. There were no console errors and it ran at a steady ~100 fps.
2. Menu (menu.png): the title, the rules and the "Press ENTER / SPACE or CLICK" prompt are clear. The controls are explained on the canvas and again in a line under it.
3. Menu bug: the icon row is crowded. The dart's trail runs over the purple hunter icon, and "Big +50" nearly touches the hazard icons. The positions were hard-coded, so the spacing doesn't follow the text width.
4. Menu wording: the line "A ! warns of incoming hazards" reads like a typo, because the "!" is plain text and not the red warning sign the game shows.
5. In play (f01, f03, f05): the HUD is readable (score/1000 with a progress bar, level, life icons, dash meter). Hazards, orbs and the ! warnings are easy to tell apart.
6. The "GRAZE +2" pop-up (f03) is small and faint, and easy to miss in a busy moment.
7. Standing still is too safe early on. Spike mines aim at random spots, never at the player, and before level 4 nothing targets you. The idle run never reached game over. Its screenshot (end.png) still shows 3 lives at level 1.
8. When a new player starts there's no reminder of the controls. You land in the playfield, and the controls are only on the menu.
9. Combos give more points but nothing else changes, so a x4 streak doesn't feel any different.
10. The bot made 110 points in 35 s. A human chaining x4 combos should reach the 1000-point win, so I left that target alone.

**Changes made**
- **Menu icon row:** it is now measured and centred, so no icons overlap.
- **Warning-sign rule:** the line now says "Red warning signs (!) mark incoming hazards".
- **Anti-camping:** about 25–40% of spike mines now head straight at you. If you stand still for over 2 s, every new mine aims at you.
- **Bigger combo reward:** the orb-pull radius now grows with the multiplier. At x4 the combo display shows "FEVER!" and your ball glows pink.
- **Controls reminder:** for the first 5 seconds of each run, a fading line at the bottom shows the controls.
- **Graze pop-up:** it is bigger and easier to see.

The `window.gameState` test hook and everything else that worked are unchanged.
